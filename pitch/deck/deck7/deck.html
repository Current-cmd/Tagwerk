Tagwerk · The problem

A site changes every hour. Its record is written from memory at night.

The site manager sees what happened. Four tools need to know, and none of them shares a key with the others.

Site diary

Bautagebuch, typed in the evening

free text

Schedule

Primavera P6, kept in the office

activity ID

Contract

GAEB bill of quantities

position no.

Claims

Notices and letters, written later

date + prose
No shared key. “Row A is installed” names no activity and no contract position, so it never reaches the schedule or the contract.
  • Progress in the schedule is an estimate, entered later in the office.
  • The forecast does not move when two men are off sick. The delay shows up at handover.
  • For a delay notice, the evidence has to be rebuilt from photos and memory.

The solution

Record once on the phone. Tagwerk links it to schedule and contract.

1

Capture

Video with commentary, photo, voice note or typed note. Pick the zone, send.

2

Understand

Speech to text; objects and markers in the frames. Sentences become states and events.

3

Link

Each statement is tied to an element, its P6 activity and its GAEB position.

4

Forecast

Remaining work is re-simulated day by day: crew minus absences, then successors.

5

Deliver

Dashboard, daily report, P6 XML, GAEB X83/X84 and .ics exports.

The next recording starts from the updated state. Camera proposals wait for one tap.

Why it is different · fit to Challenge B

The site manager stays in control. Every percent has a source.

Voice or a tap confirms. The camera only proposes.

Vision alone never changes a state; it creates a proposal. States only go up.

Every percent traces to a sentence and a contract line.

Quote, recording, element, activity and GAEB position are one click apart.

No scanner. No BIM required.

A phone is enough. When a model exists it is used: Schependomlaan, an open BIM dataset with 2,082 elements and 100 tasks.

Runs on-prem on one laptop.

Speech, vision and 3D on a 6 GB GPU. Site footage does not need to go to a cloud.

Challenge B, point by point
Documentation

Every recording is filed with zone, time, weather, transcript and what it changed.

Daily reports

The Bautagebuch for the day is written from the recordings: work done, crew present and absent, events, delays, 3-day lookahead.

Claims

Each delay carries its cause, activity and GAEB position: the basis for a delay notice under VOB/B §6. Drafting support only, not legal advice.

Status and next

What works today, and what is still open.

Works today

  • Capture pipeline end to end on the GPU laptop: video, photo, voice or note to graph and dashboard
  • Dashboard with Gantt, live graph, BIM view, 3D scan and daily report
  • Phone app (Expo, Android via Expo Go) with offline queue
  • P6 XML, GAEB X83/X84 and .ics exports
  • Two projects served: Hörsaal (seed) and Schependomlaan (open BIM data)

Open, stated plainly

  • Vision misses large surfaces and has false positives. That is why it only proposes
  • Keyword rules cannot resolve pronouns; the Claude path is tested with a fake client only
  • The Hörsaal project is placeholder seed data, not a measured site
  • The P6 XML has not been imported into a real Primavera yet
  • The delay notice itself is not generated yet; the report holds its facts

Next

  • Rehearsal on a real site, with the crew’s own words in a project glossary
  • Delay notice draft (Behinderungsanzeige), reviewed by someone who signs them
  • Standalone Android build, HTTPS, users and roles
  • Project setup from the UI instead of seed files
  • Hosting off the laptop: a GPU box in the office
